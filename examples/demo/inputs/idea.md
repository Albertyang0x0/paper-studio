# Synthetic demo: editable document triage

This is an invented onboarding example, not a real paper or completed study.

The proposed workflow extracts document metadata, checks whether required fields are present, and sends uncertain cases to a human reviewer. The reviewer may edit a proposed decision before saving it. An overview figure could show document input, metadata extraction, validation, human review, and the resulting decision record.

The implementation details and comparative benefits have not been established. Do not claim novelty, improved accuracy, or faster processing. The document is only intended to exercise the manuscript and editable-figure interfaces.
